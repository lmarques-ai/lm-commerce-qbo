Privacy Policy

LM Commerce Books Reporter · Effective November 17, 2026

Summary

This tool is used only by LM Commerce LLC, on its own QuickBooks Online company. It reads the company's own financial reports, keeps them on the company's own computer, and shares them with no one.

What data it accesses

With read-only access, the tool retrieves summary financial reports (profit and loss, balance sheet) and basic company information (company name) from LM Commerce LLC's QuickBooks Online company. It does not access data belonging to any other company or individual user.

How the data is used

Only to produce and check the company's monthly financial records.

Where it is stored

Report copies are stored on a computer owned and controlled by LM Commerce LLC. QuickBooks access tokens are stored in that computer's encrypted system keychain. The tool uses no cloud storage or outside service other than QuickBooks Online itself.

Sharing

The data is not sold, rented or shared with any third party, and is not used for advertising or analytics.

Retention and deletion

Report copies are kept as part of the company's own financial records. Disconnecting the app in QuickBooks Online (Settings → Apps) revokes access immediately; stored tokens are then useless and are deleted.

Changes

This policy may be updated; the effective date above shows the latest version.
