LM Commerce Books Reporter

A private, internal tool of LM Commerce LLC

LM Commerce Books Reporter is an internal tool that LM Commerce LLC uses with its own QuickBooks Online company. Once a month it reads the company's financial reports (profit and loss, and balance sheet), checks that they add up, and files a copy for the company's records.

It is read-only: it never creates, changes or deletes anything in QuickBooks. It is not offered to the public or to other companies.

The connection to QuickBooks is set up by LM Commerce LLC on its own computer. There is nothing to sign up for on this site.
